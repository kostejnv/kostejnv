I like the part of AI work where the shape of the solution is not obvious yet: understanding the problem, finding structure, testing approaches, and turning the useful ideas into working systems.

At Outfindo, I build applied AI for e-commerce product discovery: customer insight extraction, NLP/LLM pipelines, recommendation logic, product data workflows, and AI-generated product content. I care about systems that make complex information easier to use, not about AI novelty for its own sake.

## Selected work

- [`mapy-com-mcp`](https://github.com/kostejnv/mapy-com-mcp) - an unofficial MCP server for mapy.com: geocoding, routing, static maps, and shareable map links for LLM workflows.
- [`GRS-fairness`](https://github.com/kostejnv/GRS-fairness) - SAGEA, a sparse-autoencoder approach to fairness-preserving group recommendation. RecSys 2025 LBR and my master's thesis work.
- [`prithvi_segmentation`](https://github.com/kostejnv/prithvi_segmentation) - U-Prithvi, integrating a GeoAI foundation model with U-Net for flood inundation mapping. GIScience 2025.

## Interests

- Applied AI systems from early problem framing to working implementation.
- Recommender systems, fairness, interpretability, optimization, and human behavior patterns.
- NLP and LLM-based pipelines that extract useful structure from messy information.
- Production-oriented ML engineering with Python, FastAPI, Docker, Kubernetes, GitHub Actions, and MLflow.

I studied AI and computer science at the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics, Charles University, with additional research experience at North Carolina State University.
